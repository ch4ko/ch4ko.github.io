# About Me

Hi! I'm a cybersecurity enthusiast focused on penetration testing, ethical hacking, and information security.

I enjoy exploring security vulnerabilities, understanding how systems work, and improving my practical skills through hands-on labs and continuous learning.

This blog is where I document my learning journey, share technical notes, and write about techniques and lessons I've learned along the way.
